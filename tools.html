Pace count

What changes your pace count

SlopesYour pace lengthens going downhill and shortens going uphill.
WindA headwind shortens your pace. A tailwind lengthens it.
SurfaceSand, gravel, mud, and snow shorten your pace.
WeatherSnow, rain, and ice shorten your pace.
Clothing and loadExtra clothing, a heavy load, and boots with poor traction shorten your pace.
VisibilityFog, rain, or darkness shortens your pace.

The manual gives no fixed correction for these. Walk the course in each condition and use your own numbers.
