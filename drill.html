Grid drill

Find the grid coordinate of the red point. Read right, then up.

How many digits

How to read it

  1. Find the grid line to the left of the point. Its number is the first two digits.
  2. Find the grid line below the point. Its number is the next two digits. That is the 4-digit grid.
  3. For 6 digits, count the 100 m divisions to the right of the left line, then up from the bottom line.
  4. For 8 digits, do the same again with the 10 m divisions.
  5. Never round up. Use the division the point has already passed.
